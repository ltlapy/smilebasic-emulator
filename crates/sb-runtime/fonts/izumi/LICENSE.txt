Izumi Bitmap Font Series: Izumi Gothic 16
Designed by Yu Izumi (Yu=Izumi), 2001-2007.

The author declares the font equivalent to public domain: anyone may use,
change and redistribute it, commercially or not, with no duty to credit
the font or to contact the author. The author asks, without requiring it,
to hear about changes and ports. The texts below are the author's own,
unchanged; the English summary above is this project's.
